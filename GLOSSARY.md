# Dashulya

Dashulya assists its owner in private conversations and observed Telegram groups.

## Language

**Conversation**:
The exchange between the owner and Dashulya within one chat and, when present,
one topic. Clearing a conversation starts a fresh exchange in that same place.

**Group registry**:
The groups Dashulya has observed itself belonging to, with their latest known
identities and names. It can be incomplete after missed Telegram updates.
_Avoid_: Complete chat list

**Addressing decision**:
The determination that a group message invites Dashulya to answer. An explicit
mention or reply establishes this directly; a name reference needs interpretation.

**Chat memory**:
Historical messages and source-backed knowledge belonging to one Telegram chat,
available for recalling its discussions, people, tools and projects.
_Avoid_: Live Telegram history

**Memory access scope**:
The originating chats whose memory a conversation is allowed to recall.

**Chat history**:
The original messages available from one Telegram chat, with their senders,
send times and reply relationships, within the known coverage.

**Reply context**:
The messages from a conversation selected to understand one current request,
including the earlier messages needed to interpret its replies and references.

**Accumulated chat knowledge**:
Source-supported information about a chat's people, tools, projects and discussion
outcomes, with explicit statements, attributed opinions and agent observations
kept distinguishable.

**Agent observation**:
An agent's interpretation of a recurring pattern in a chat, supported by several
original messages and distinct from a participant's explicit statement.

**Discussion outcome**:
A decision, agreement or unresolved disagreement arising from a chat discussion,
with identifiable participants, time and original supporting messages.

**Historical change**:
A real change in a person's circumstances or a tool/project's state between
different times, such as moving from one tool to another.

**Knowledge correction**:
A revision of accumulated chat knowledge because an earlier claim was mistaken,
rather than because its subject changed over time.
