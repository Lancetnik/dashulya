# Dashulya

Dashulya assists its owner in private conversations and observed Telegram groups.

## Language

**Conversation**:
The exchange between the owner and Dashulya within one chat and, when present,
one topic. Clearing a conversation starts a fresh exchange in that same place.

**Group registry**:
The groups Dashulya has observed itself belonging to, with their latest known
identities and names. It can be incomplete after missed Telegram updates.
_Avoid_: Complete chat list

**Addressing decision**:
The determination that a group message invites Dashulya to answer. An explicit
mention or reply establishes this directly; a name reference needs interpretation.

**Chat memory**:
Historical messages and source-backed derived records available for recalling
earlier discussions. Its coverage is limited to the imported snapshot.
_Avoid_: Live Telegram history
